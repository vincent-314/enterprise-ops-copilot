# Onboarding Devices & Baseline Access

## Standard Provisioning Checklist
1. Corporate email, unified SSO authentication, and internal Wiki read permissions are provisioned automatically on Day 1.
2. GitLab repository and Jira issue-tracker access require your direct mentor to select the target project group in the Access Center for automated approval.
3. Read-only access to the production log platform requires passing the *Production Security & Compliance Exam* prior to submitting an access request.
